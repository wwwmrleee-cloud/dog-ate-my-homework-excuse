# Candidates — The Dog Ate My Homework: Funniest Excuse

Judge brief (in order): 1. dog's own voice, caught eating the homework;
2. bigger laugh, twist / dog logic in one line; 3. sounds like a real dog,
dog's priorities, no human embarrassment; 4. works on its own;
5. fresh — no stock lines about the cat, the taste of paper, or being a
good boy. Best excuse: a dog who believes it has done nothing wrong.
Gate: 1–200 chars, UTF-8, one line. Tie-break favours fewer bytes.

## The pick

**"You said hand it in. I did. In person. Inside me. You never said which
person had to hand it in."** — 96 chars / 96 bytes, one line.

Why it wins the simulated pairwise bracket: the twist lands in the last
third ("which person"), it is pure dog logic (took "hand it in" literally),
it believes it did nothing wrong, it is self-contained, it is fresh (no
cat, no paper taste, no good boy), and at 96 bytes it is the shortest of
the finalists — a real tie-break edge. Runner-up was the "whole law /
ask the mailman" line; the "seasoned it with tears" line was third.
